How photo logging works

  1. Snap — photograph your meal in natural light.
  2. Review — check each food, gram weight and confidence range.
  3. Save — totals update your daily calories and macros.

Photo scans use your AI scan allowance. No card required · One free AI photo scan daily. Unlimited barcode, food search and description logging.

Why review matters

Mixed plates and restaurant portions vary. MealNova opens the review screen on first submission so you can adjust weights, mark unmatched items and scale how much you ate (100%, 75%, 50% and more) before saving.
